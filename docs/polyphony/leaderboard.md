# Leaderboard — every competition, recorded

Polyphony selects by **adversarial competition against real data**, never by clearing a fixed
threshold. This page is the running record of **every tournament**: the contenders, the metric,
the out-of-sample / holdout / distribution-shift result, and **why the winner won**. A feature,
coupling, model, or method is only "in" if it appears here as a **winner over named rivals** — and
it can be **dethroned** by a later challenger or a red-team break.

!!! info "Reading a row"
    Each contest records: **question** (what is being predicted/decided), **contenders**,
    **data & split** (dataset, train/test window, shift test), **metric(s)**, **winner**,
    **margin**, **why**, **red-team status**, and a link to the eval script + ADR.

Machine-readable results live in **[`leaderboard.json`](leaderboard.json)**, regenerated by
`python -m polyphony.experiments.run_leaderboard`.

## Round 1 — first synergy tournament (method validation)

The first real contest asks: **does coupling energy⇄climate⇄economy earn its keep?** A **coupled**
4-voice ensemble (energy + climate + CGE + E3ME) is scored against an **economy-only** baseline
(the same economics voices with the feedback switched off — the "sum-of-parts") on a **held-out,
time-blocked** GDP track. Run on **two synthetic regimes**: one whose data-generating process *has*
the coupling, and a **negative control** where GDP is independent of emissions.

| Question | Champion | Beat | Metric | Value | Synergy Δ | Data | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| GDP — **coupled** regime (DGP has energy⇄climate⇄economy feedback) | coupled ensemble | economy-only | MASE, held-out 30% | 8.016 | **+2.216** | synthetic-policy n=40 | coupled tracks the damage-driven decline; Δ>0 ⇒ **keep** |
| GDP — **decoupled** regime (negative control: GDP ⟂ emissions) | economy-only | coupled ensemble | MASE, held-out 30% | 0.599 | **−1.727** | synthetic-decoupled n=40 | coupled wrongly imposes damages; economy-only wins ⇒ **cut** |

**What this establishes — and what it does not.** The method **detects synergy when the coupling is
real (Δ>0, keep) and correctly cuts it when spurious (Δ≤0)**. That validates Polyphony's core
*mechanism* — the falsifiable synergy test — on data where the truth is known. It is **not** a claim
of real-world predictive skill: both MASE values are >1 (the toys are uncalibrated to the synthetic
levels), and the target is **synthetic** (real data is [issue #9](https://github.com/Sour-abh-Raj/computational-policy-atlas/issues/9);
network fetch is currently blocked). See [04-validation](04-validation.md) for the full honesty
accounting.

## Round 2 — Red Team (does the champion survive attack?)

The Round-1 champion (the coupled ensemble) was attacked with five stress tests
(`polyphony/tournament/redteam.py`): distribution shift, a **Lucas-critique** policy-regime change,
extreme dials, noise-seed instability, and a **naive-baseline** skill test.

| Attack | Result | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| distribution_shift | survived | coupling advantage persists under a mis-assumed carbon price |
| lucas_regime_change | survived | advantage persists across a mid-series policy shift |
| edge_dials | survived | predictions stay finite at extreme carbon_price/tcre |
| noise_stability | survived | synergy sign holds across all noise seeds (flip fraction 0) |
| **naive_baseline** | **BROKE** | coupled **MASE ≈ 8 > 1** — the champion loses to a naive random-walk forecast |

**Verdict: the champion does NOT survive.** The decisive finding — surfaced by the Red Team, not
hidden — is that the coupling's "synergy" is only relative to an **artificially weak** economy-only
baseline; on absolute skill the reduced-form champion is **worse than naive**. So there is **no skill
claim** yet: the synergy *method* is validated, the *champion* is not. This break feeds back as a
hard gate — calibration + real data (issue #9) are prerequisites before any champion can be trusted.

## Contest log

### Round 0 — (Phase 0)
Inventory & positioning; no tournaments. The contests race:

1. **Feature competition** — candidate features/transformations for the DICE⇄energy⇄CGE slice,
   scored on out-of-sample backtests with overfitting/leakage/redundancy penalties.
2. **Coupling/synergy competition** — coupled configurations vs decoupled baselines vs rival
   couplings; a coupling advances only if it adds **demonstrable synergy** on real data.
3. **Model/method competition** — rival paradigm voices head-to-head on the same question; route
   to winners; where winners disagree, **keep both and report the disagreement**.
4. **Ensembling competition** — Bayesian model averaging **vs** deliberate disagreement-preservation.

Each will be logged here with full provenance so any collaborator can reproduce and challenge it.

## Convergence criterion (the DONE state)

A **stable champion** that no challenger feature, coupling, method, or red-team attack can beat
within a full tournament round — competition **converged** — **and** net positive synergy over
isolated baselines on real data, across all covered domains. Until then, the loop continues.
